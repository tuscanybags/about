# Tuscany Bags Japan

Tuscany Bags is an authorised retailer of Tuscany Leather, offering Italian leather bags and accessories to customers across Japan. Visit the official website: [Tuscany Bags](https://tuscanybags.jp).

## About Tuscany Bags

Tuscany Bags offers women's handbags, totes, crossbody and shoulder bags, men's briefcases and laptop bags, backpacks, travel bags and holdalls, wallets, and leather accessories.

The collection features leather goods made in Florence, Italy, following the Florentine tradition of vegetable tanning, cutting, and stitching. Prices are in Japanese yen (JPY).
